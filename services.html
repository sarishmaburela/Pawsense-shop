Features

Four ways Pawsense helps you look after your pet, all from one smart collar.

Live GPS tracking

Follow your pet’s location in real time and set safe zones around home, the park or the groomer.

Health monitoring

Keep an eye on heart rate and rest patterns, and share them easily with your vet.

Hunger detection

Get a reminder when your pet is likely to be hungry, based on their usual routine.

Activity analytics

See how much your pet walks, plays and sleeps, and set goals for healthier days.
