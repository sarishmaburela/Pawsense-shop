Built by pet people.

Pawsense began with a simple question: what if our pets could tell us how they’re doing? We’re a small pet-tech team making a collar that listens, so you can spend less time worrying and more time playing.

What we believe

Technology should feel as calm and comfortable as a favourite blanket. Every part of Pawsense is designed to be light, quiet and kind to pets.

Comfort first

A soft, lightweight collar your pet forgets they’re wearing.

Clear, not noisy

Simple updates that tell you what matters, when it matters.

Your pet’s data is yours

We only use health and location data to help you care for your pet.
